# Privacy Policy for AnimaPic AI

**Effective Date: May 2024**

Welcome to AnimaPic AI (hereinafter referred to as "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data to support your AI artistic creation experience.

### 1. Information We Collect
- **Identity and Account**: When you sign in via Apple ID or email, we collect unique identifiers to synchronize your creation history, favorites, **VIP/SVIP status, and diamond balance**.
- **Creation Data**: We collect the prompts you enter, reference images, and the resulting AI-generated images/videos. This data is encrypted and stored in the cloud for cross-device access.
- **Device and Logs**: To ensure service stability, we collect device models, OS versions, and crash logs.
- **Payment Data**: All In-App Purchases (IAP) are processed by Apple. We only record transaction IDs and status to ensure your credits are delivered. **We do not directly collect or store your payment card information.**

### 2. How We Use Information
- **Core AI Services**: To transmit your instructions to AI engines for content generation.
- **Cloud Sync**: To ensure your inspirations are saved permanently and accessible across devices.
- **Compliance**: We perform automated scans on user-generated content to comply with local laws and safety guidelines.

### 3. Third-Party Service Providers
To provide top-tier generation results, we collaborate with providers such as **OpenRouter (Gemini/MiniMax), Pollinations, and Hugging Face**.
- When you submit a task, relevant text prompts or images are encrypted and sent to these providers.
- we do not share your name, email, or payment information with these third parties.

### 4. Data Security and Rights
- **Security**: We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS, encrypted databases) to prevent unauthorized access.
- **Your Rights**: You can access and export your history at any time. You may also choose to **Delete Account** in Settings, which will permanently erase all your cloud data.

---
If you have any questions, please contact us via the official feedback channel.
