# Privacy Policy for AIDream

**Effective Date: June 2026**

Welcome to AIDream (referred to as "we", "us", or "our"). We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data to support your AI creative experience.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

- **Anonymous Device Identifier**: To protect your benefits (such as Premium subscription status and diamond balance), we generate a random anonymous identifier securely stored in your device's iOS Keychain. **This app does not require account registration and does not collect your name, phone number, or email address.**
- **Creation Data**: We process the text prompts, reference images, and the resulting AI-generated images and videos you submit. This data is used solely to complete generation tasks.
- **Device & Logs**: To ensure service stability, we collect device model, OS version, and crash logs.
- **Payment Data**: All In-App Purchases (IAP) are processed by Apple. We only record transaction IDs and status to ensure your subscription and diamond credits are correctly delivered. **We do not directly collect or store your payment card information.**
- **Firebase Remote Config**: We use Firebase Remote Config to deliver app configuration parameters. This process does not collect personally identifiable information.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

- **Core AI Services**: We encrypt and transmit your prompts or reference images to AI engines (see Section 3) to generate images or videos.
- **Local Storage**: Your creation history, favorites, and diamond balance are stored primarily on your device (Keychain and UserDefaults) and are not uploaded to our servers.
- **Content Safety**: We perform automated scans on user-generated content (UGC) that passes through our infrastructure to prevent the spread of illegal or harmful content.

---

### 3. Third-Party AI Service Providers

To deliver high-quality AI generation results, AIDream works with the following third-party providers. When you submit a generation task, relevant text prompts or images are encrypted and sent to the corresponding provider:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Pollinations** | AI image generation |
| **Hugging Face** (Stable Video Diffusion) | AI video generation |
| **OpenRouter** (Gemini 2.5 Flash / MiniMax Hailuo) | AI image and video generation |
| **Google Firebase** | Remote configuration delivery |

We do not share any personally identifiable information with these providers. Each provider maintains its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review on their respective websites.

---

### 4. Your Rights: Data Control (App Store Compliance)

You have full control over your local data:

- **Access Your History**: You can view all your generated works in the "History" tab at any time.
- **Clear All Data**: You can select **"Clear All Data"** in Profile → Settings. Once confirmed, we will immediately and **permanently delete** all your anonymous identifiers, diamond balance, Premium subscription records, favorites, and creation history from your device's secure storage. **This action is irreversible. Since there is no account system, deleted data cannot be recovered.**

---

### 5. Children's Privacy

AIDream is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used this app, please contact us via the in-app feedback channel.

---

### 6. Content Reporting

As an AI-generated content (AIGC) application, we strictly oppose harmful content. If you encounter inappropriate AI-generated content, please use the "Report" button on the video or image detail page. Our moderation team will review and take action within 24 hours.

---

### 7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be communicated through an in-app notice or by updating the effective date above.

---

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us via the in-app feedback channel.
