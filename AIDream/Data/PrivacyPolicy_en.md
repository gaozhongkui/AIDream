# Privacy Policy for AnimaPic AI

**Effective Date: May 2024**

Welcome to AnimaPic AI (hereinafter referred to as "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we handle your data to support your AI artistic creation experience.

### 1. Information We Collect
- **Anonymous Identifiers**: To protect your benefits (such as VIP subscriptions and diamond balance), we generate a random anonymous identifier safely stored in your device's iOS Keychain. **This app does not require account registration and does not collect your name, phone number, or email.**
- **Creation Data**: We process the prompts you enter, reference images, and the resulting AI-generated videos/images. This data is processed only to complete the generation tasks.
- **Device and Logs**: To ensure service stability, we collect device models, OS versions, and crash logs.
- **Payment Data**: All In-App Purchases (IAP) are processed by Apple. We only record transaction IDs and status to ensure your credits are delivered. **We do not directly collect or store your payment card information.**

### 2. How We Use Information
- **Core AI Services**: To transmit your instructions to AI engines for content generation.
- **Local Storage**: Your creation history and favorites are stored primarily on your local device.
- **Compliance**: We perform automated scans on user-generated content to prevent the spread of illegal or harmful content.

### 3. Third-Party Service Providers
To provide top-tier generation results, we collaborate with leading AI providers such as **OpenRouter, Pollinations, and Hugging Face**.
- When you submit a task, relevant text prompts or images are encrypted and sent to these providers.
- We do not share any personal identifying information with these third parties.

### 4. Your Rights: Data Control (App Store Compliance)
You have absolute control over your local data:
- **Access History**: You can view all your generated works in "Creative History".
- **Clear All Data**: You can choose **"Clear All Data"** in Settings. Once confirmed, we will immediately and **permanently delete** all your anonymous identifiers, diamond balance, favorites, and creation history from local secure storage. This action is irreversible, and since there is no account system, data cannot be recovered after deletion.

### 5. Content Reporting and Feedback
As an AIGC application, we strictly oppose harmful content. If you find inappropriate AI-generated content, please use the "Report" button on the video details page. Our moderation team will review and take action within 24 hours.

---
If you have any questions, please contact us via the official feedback channel.
